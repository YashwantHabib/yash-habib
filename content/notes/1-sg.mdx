# From Intern to Software Engineer

I joined Scientific Games as an intern, not really knowing what to expect. Like most students coming out of engineering, I thought I had a decent understanding of software development.

I was wrong.

In those few months, I probably learned more than I did during my four years of engineering. Asking questions, making mistakes, and learning from experienced engineers taught me things that no classroom ever could.

Somewhere along the way, the internship turned into a full-time opportunity.

Today, I'm an Associate Software Engineer at Scientific Games. I'm still at the beginning of my journey, and there's a lot I don't know yet—and I actually like that. It means there's always something new to learn, another problem to solve, or another rabbit hole to disappear into.

Looking back, joining Scientific Games has been one of the biggest turning points in my career so far. I'm grateful for everything I've learned, excited about everything I haven't.


I'm grateful to everyone at Scientific Games who made this journey possible, and I'm excited for everything that's still ahead.
